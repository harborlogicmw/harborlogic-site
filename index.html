The Problem

100 days to make the year.
Zero room for chaos.

Seasonal businesses have a problem nobody talks about: institutional amnesia. Every spring you hire a new crew, teach them everything, survive the summer — and every fall, what they learned leaves with them. You're the only continuity. That's why you can't step away, can't scale, and can't stop.

It's not a staffing problem. It's a systems problem. And it's fixable.

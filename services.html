What I Do

Four kinds of work. One idea underneath.

Every engagement starts the same way: find what repeats, find where decisions get stuck, and decide what should stay human. What gets built after that depends on the business.

Operating systems

The knowledge layer

SOPs, checklists, training, workflows, and the business rules nobody has written down. One place your team goes for the answer instead of coming to you. Harbor OS is this work packaged for seasonal operators; the same approach fits a business that runs year round.

AI workflows

Where AI earns its place

Which repeated work AI genuinely shortens, and which it doesn’t. Connected to business knowledge you’ve approved, with human review wherever the decision matters. No stack rebuild, and no AI at all where documentation or a better process is the honest answer.

Reporting

Numbers you actually look at

Getting the figures out of the systems they’re trapped in and onto something you read on a Monday. Built in the tools you already pay for — spreadsheets first, Power BI or Tableau when the data earns it.

Fractional operations

A second operator

Ongoing support without a full-time hire: planning, process, and the follow-through that keeps a system alive after the project ends. Most systems don’t fail at the build. They fail six months later.

Harbor OS has published pricing. Everything else is scoped at the audit, before anyone commits to anything.
Why an operator

I didn’t learn this
from a framework.

I spent 20 years in destination hospitality — Colorado ski country, then Northern Michigan resort towns and an island resort — running food and beverage operations with 250+ seasonal staff. My title said operations. What I actually did was build the structure underneath: what got written down, what got trained, and how fast someone found the answer without coming to me.

Most consultants arrive with a framework and fit your business to it. I start with how the work already moves, because I have been the person the questions come to at 6pm on a Saturday.

I don’t sell AI. I sell the hours it gives you back.
